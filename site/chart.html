Tick Chart Trader

Fake money. Prices update tick by tick. By default it uses real prices from Yahoo Finance (free, no keys; unofficial and possibly a little delayed, and the bid/ask spread is estimated). With your trader passcode it can use Alpaca's real quotes instead (needs Alpaca keys on the server), or you can switch to simulated ticks. Buys fill at the ask and sells at the bid, so the spread costs you, just like a real market. The bot follows the skills' rules (trader-risk: 10% cash buffer, 25% max position, $10 minimum, daily order cap) plus the risk rules day-trading research and educators agree on: risk at most 1% of equity per trade, sized from the stop distance, and stop for the day after a 2% loss. None of these strategies has reliable evidence of beating the market after costs. Not financial advice.

Backtest: does it actually work?

Runs the exact same rules and filters as the bot over past 1-minute bars, with trading costs, and compares each strategy with simply buying and holding. Yahoo gives about 7 days of 1-minute history; Alpaca (with the passcode) up to 45. On simulated data the result only shows what costs do to random prices.
