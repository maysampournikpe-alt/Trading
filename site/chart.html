Tick Chart Trader

Fake money. Prices update tick by tick. By default the ticks are simulated; enter your trader passcode to stream real quotes and 1-minute bars from Alpaca (IEX feed, needs the Alpaca keys on the server). Buys fill at the ask and sells at the bid, so the spread costs you, just like a real market. The bot follows the skills' rules: trader-risk limits (10% cash buffer, 25% max position, $10 minimum, daily order cap) and trader-budget sizing (20% of equity per trade). Not financial advice.
