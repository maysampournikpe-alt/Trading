Paper trading by default

Trade like the people you trust, with a seatbelt.

Eleven Claude skills that copy investors from their SEC filings, watch the news and Congress, size every trade to your budget, and never place an order without your explicit yes.

Open the paper trading desk (sign up to save it) Tick chart trader

The eleven

Reverse Engineer

/trader-copycat

Rebuilds an investor's portfolio from their 13F filing, with what they bought, trimmed and sold.

News Monitor

/trader-news

Headlines and 8-K filings for the stocks you hold, shown once and flagged by relevance.

Budget Manager

/trader-budget

Sizes positions to your money with a cash buffer and a per-stock cap.

Pelosi Tracker

/trader-pelosi

Reads House STOCK Act disclosures as they post and turns each PDF into clean rows.

Earnings Analyst

/trader-analyst

Margins, EPS and growth quarter by quarter, straight from SEC EDGAR.

Stock Screener

/trader-screener

Checks companies against your own rules and says which rule each one fails.

Price Alerts

/trader-alerts

Targets, stops, percent moves and the NYSE opening bell. Each rings once.

Rebalancer

/trader-rebalance

Trades only the stocks that drift outside your band, sells listed before buys.

Risk Manager

/trader-risk

Blocks any order that breaks your limits before it can reach the broker.

Tax Optimizer

/trader-tax

Cost-basis lots, short vs long term, loss harvesting and wash-sale checks. Not tax advice.

Order Executor

/trader-broker

Shows each order as a ticket and records your yes before your broker connector is called.

How it flows

  1. Copy or screen: pick an investor or shortlist stocks.
  2. Size: the budget skill turns weights into dollar orders. Nothing is placed.
  3. Check: the risk skill passes or blocks every order.
  4. Approve: you say yes to each order, then Claude's own Allow once card asks again.

Install

/plugin marketplace add Jakeschincariol/trader-skill
/plugin install trader-skill@trader-skill

Or copy the trader-* folders into .claude/skills/. Python 3.8+, nothing to pip install. Set SEC_USER_AGENT to your name and email for SEC requests.
