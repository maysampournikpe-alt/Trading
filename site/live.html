Live Trader

A fast short-term trader on your Alpaca account, using real-time quotes (IEX feed) every 2 seconds, with the trader-risk and trader-budget limits applied to every order.

Paper by default; real money when the server is set to live. Fast trading is not a reliable way to make money: most short-term traders lose, spreads and the IEX-only feed work against you, and a profitable day here proves nothing. Runs only while this tab is open. Not financial advice.

Every buy is checked on the server: the stock must be an active, tradable US stock on a listed exchange (no OTC), at least $5 a share, and support dollar-amount orders. Positions in stocks you remove from the list are left alone; use Sell everything to close them.

Enter the passcode you set as TRADER_PASSCODE on the server.
